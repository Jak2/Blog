Diets formulated for sensitive digestion prioritize bioavailable proteins, soluble and insoluble fiber balances, and active microbiome support to reduce gastrointestinal strain.

**Essential Dietary Components**

* **Easily Digestible Proteins:** Lean meats such as skinless chicken, turkey, lamb, and novel proteins like salmon or duck minimize gastrointestinal irritation and allergy flare-ups.
* **Gentle Carbohydrates:** Brown rice, white rice, cooked oatmeal, and pumpkin provide non-abrasive energy sources and assist in firming loose stool.
* **Prebiotic and Probiotic Additions:** Ingredients like chicory root, inulin, beet pulp, and live cultures (such as *Lactobacillus* or *Enterococcus*) replenish beneficial gut flora.
* **Omega-3 Fatty Acids:** Sourced from salmon oil or flaxseed to reduce systemic gut inflammation.

**Ingredients to Limit or Avoid**

* Unspecified meat by-products and rendered meals.
* Common trigger grains like wheat, corn, and soy when sensitivities are present.
* Excessively high dietary fats, which can trigger acute pancreatitis or loose stools.
* Artificial additives, artificial colors, chemical preservatives (such as BHA, BHT, or ethoxyquin), and synthetic flavorings.
