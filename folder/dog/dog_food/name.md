file # Canine Nutrition and Dietary Management Report

### Executive Summary

Formulating an optimal canine feeding regimen requires precise alignment with age, breed size, metabolic activity, and ingredient quality. A nutritionally complete diet sustains musculoskeletal integrity, digestive efficiency, and immune resilience throughout all life stages.

---

### Nutritional Assessment Framework

| Parameter | Recommended Specification | Primary Function |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Crude Protein** | 22% to 38% dry matter basis | Muscle maintenance and tissue repair |
| **Healthy Fats** | 10% to 20% (Omega-3 and Omega-6) | Cellular integrity and coat vitality |
| **Fiber Sources** | 2% to 5% digestible prebiotic fiber | Gastrointestinal motility and microbiome health |
| **Mineral Balance** | Controlled Calcium:Phosphorus ratio (1.2:1) | Skeletal density and joint preservation |

---

### Key Dietary Categories and Product Evaluations

**Whole-Prey and High-Protein Formulations**

* **Orijen Original / Six Fish:** Delivers up to 85% animal-derived ingredients. Suited for active adult dogs requiring concentrated nutrient density without grain fillers.
* **Taste of the Wild (Wetlands / Pacific Stream):** Incorporates roasted fowl or smoked salmon paired with species-specific K9 probiotics to optimize absorption for dogs with sensitive digestion.

**Low-Glycemic and Ancestral Grain Diets**

* **Farmina N&D Ancestral Grain (Chicken & Pomegranate):** Balances 60% animal proteins with 20% ancestral grains (spelt and oats). Provides sustained energy release while minimizing glycemic spikes.
* **Farmina N&D Pumpkin Selection:** Replaces traditional cereals with pumpkin-derived soluble fiber to support gut regularity.

**Breed-Size Targeted Formulations**

* **Royal Canin Maxi Adult:** Formulated with chondroitin, glucosamine, and EPA-DHA to mitigate joint wear in large breeds (26 kg to 44 kg).
* **Royal Canin Medium Adult:** Focuses on antioxidant support and adapted calorie density for moderate-mass working and companion breeds.

---

### Standard Dietary Transition Protocol

Dietary transitions require a structured 10-day integration schedule to maintain gastrointestinal stability:

* **Days 1 to 3:** 75% baseline diet blended with 25% target diet.
* **Days 4 to 6:** 50% baseline diet blended with 50% target diet.
* **Days 7 to 9:** 25% baseline diet blended with 75% target diet.
* **Day 10 onward:** 100% target diet fully established.

---

### Prohibited Ingredients and Hazard Management

Certain foods contain compounds toxic to canine physiology and must remain entirely excluded from daily rations:

* **Methylxanthines:** Found in cocoa and chocolate; causes cardiac arrhythmia.
* **Allium Species:** Onions, garlic, and leeks; causes hemolytic anemia.
* **Tartaric Acid:** Present in grapes and raisins; risks acute renal failure.
* **Xylitol / Birch Extract:** Induces rapid hypoglycemia and hepatic necrosis.

---

### Strategic Recommendation

For highly active dogs, whole-prey protein recipes like Orijen or Farmina Ancestral Grain offer superior amino acid profiles. For large breeds prone to orthopedic stress, breed-specific diets containing targeted joint-support compounds provide the most balanced long-term health outcomes. Feeding volumes should remain indexed to regular body condition scoring (BCS) and veterinarian oversight.
