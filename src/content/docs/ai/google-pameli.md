# Google Pomelli

## Overview

Note on naming: no Google AI product called "Pameli" exists. This is almost certainly **Pomelli**, an experimental AI marketing tool launched by Google Labs and Google DeepMind in late 2025 — the name is the closest real match and is used throughout this doc.

## What is it?

Pomelli is a web-based marketing assistant for small and medium-sized businesses. Point it at your business website and it builds a "Business DNA" profile — your brand's colors, fonts, image style, and tone of voice — then generates campaign ideas and branded assets (social posts, ads, website copy, photoshoots, brand books, animated assets) from that profile.

## Why use it?

- Small businesses without a marketing/design team can get on-brand campaign assets quickly and cheaply.
- Removes the "start from a blank brief" problem — Pomelli proposes campaign ideas itself, based on your actual site.
- Keeps output visually consistent with your existing brand instead of generic AI-generated content.

## How to Access

- Public beta via [Google Labs](https://labs.google) / [Pomelli](https://pomelli.google) (English only, as of the beta: US, Canada, Australia, New Zealand).
- Free during the beta/testing phase.
- No install — browser-based; sign in with a Google account.

## Basic Usage

1. Go to Pomelli and enter your business's website URL.
2. Pomelli analyzes the site and builds a "Business DNA" profile (colors, fonts, imagery, tone).
3. Review AI-suggested campaign ideas, or write your own prompt.
4. Generate branded assets (posts, ads, site copy, etc.).
5. Edit and download assets for use across channels.

## Key Features

- Automatic "Business DNA" extraction from an existing website.
- AI-suggested campaign ideas tailored to the business.
- Multi-format asset generation: social posts, ads, website copy, brand books, photoshoots, animated assets.
- Editable output before download.
- Newer agentic capabilities added post-launch (per Google's blog) for more autonomous campaign generation.

## Top 5 Use Cases

1. A small business generating a month of on-brand social posts without a designer.
2. Quickly producing ad creative for a new promotion.
3. Building a lightweight brand book/style guide from an existing site.
4. Generating product photoshoot-style imagery without a physical shoot.
5. Testing campaign concepts cheaply before committing budget to a full agency.

## Competitors

- Canva (Magic Design / brand kits)
- Adobe Express / Firefly
- Jasper AI (marketing copy)
- HubSpot's AI content tools

## Pros

- Free during beta.
- Genuinely brand-consistent output, thanks to the Business DNA step.
- Covers multiple asset types from one tool (copy + visuals).
- Backed by DeepMind — likely to keep improving quickly.

## Cons

- Beta stage: English-only, limited to four countries as of launch.
- Being a Labs product, it's experimental — could change, gate features, or be discontinued.
- Name ambiguity ("Pameli") suggests it isn't yet a broadly recognized/marketed product — worth confirming current status before relying on it professionally.
