**[ripienaar/free-for-dev](https://github.com/ripienaar/free-for-dev)**

## Overview

Free-for-Dev is a curated list of SaaS, PaaS, and IaaS offerings with genuine free tiers — targeted at infrastructure developers, DevOps practitioners, and sysadmins looking to build without a budget.

## What is it?

A category-organized reference (50+ categories: cloud providers, CI/CD, monitoring/logging, APIs and data services, databases, storage, developer tools/IDEs, communication platforms, and more) that only includes as-a-service offerings — not self-hosted software — meeting a bar of a genuinely useful free tier lasting at least a year, with basic security standards considered.

## Why use it?

Free tiers change constantly and marketing pages rarely make the real limits clear. Free-for-Dev filters for offerings that are actually usable long-term (not a 14-day trial) and organizes them by category, so evaluating "what's the free option for X" takes a lookup instead of researching pricing pages across a dozen vendors.

N/A — it's a curated reference list. Browse the README by category.

## Basic Usage

Open the repo, jump to the category you need (e.g. "CI/CD," "Monitoring," "Database"), and check the listed free-tier services and their limits.

## Key Features

- 50+ categories spanning cloud, CI/CD, monitoring, APIs, databases, dev tools, communication
- Filters specifically for as-a-service (not self-hosted) offerings
- Requires genuine free tiers lasting a year or longer, not short trials
- Considers security standards of the free tier, not just cost
- Actively maintained with 1,600+ contributors

## Top 5 Use Cases

1. Finding free-tier infrastructure for a side project or MVP
2. Evaluating cost-effective options before committing to paid tiers
3. Bootstrapping a startup's infra stack on genuinely free services
4. Sourcing free CI/CD, monitoring, or database services for open-source projects
5. Researching what free-tier landscape exists for a given service category
